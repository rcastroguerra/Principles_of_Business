# **Question:** 1

**Question:**  
A specialized area of management that oversees the process of transforming resources into goods and services is called:

**Options:**  
- marketing management  
- operations management  
- inventory management  
- financial management  

---

**Correct Answer:**  
**operations management**

---

**Explanation:**  
**Operations management** is the area responsible for overseeing the process of **transforming resources (such as raw materials, labor, and technology)** into **finished goods and services**.  

- **Marketing management** focuses on promoting and selling products.  
- **Inventory management** deals only with controlling stock levels.  
- **Financial management** handles a company’s financial resources.  

Therefore, the only option that covers the **entire transformation process** is **operations management**.
