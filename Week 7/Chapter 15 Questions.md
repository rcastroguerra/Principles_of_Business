# **Question:** 1

## Multiple Choice Question

The study of money and how it is managed by individuals, companies, and governments is known as:

A. Banking  
B. Finance  
C. Exchange  
D. Accounting  

## Correct Answer: **B. Finance**

### Explanation:

**Finance** is the field that studies money, investments, budgeting, borrowing, saving, and how funds are managed by people, businesses, and governments.

### Why the others are incorrect:

- **A. Banking** → Refers specifically to banks and financial services, not the full study of money management.  
- **C. Exchange** → Usually refers to trading or swapping goods, services, or currencies.  
- **D. Accounting** → Focuses on recording and reporting financial transactions, not the broader management of money.
